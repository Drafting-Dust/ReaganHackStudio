The Studio

Considered interiors, built to last.

Reagan Hack Studio is an interior architecture and design practice with a parallel passion for art curation.

Every project begins with how a space should feel and how people will move through it. We work closely with each client, translating the way they live or work into floor plans, finishes and furniture that feel inevitable rather than decorated.

Drawing on a technical foundation in Revit and CAD and high fidelity visualization through Enscape and D5 Render, the studio lets clients see and walk a room long before it is built. Art is never an afterthought. It is curated alongside the architecture so the finished interior reads as one complete idea.

What we do

Capabilities

01

Interior Architecture

Spatial planning, layouts, joinery and built form. We reshape how a space works at the structural level, documented to build standard in Revit and CAD.

02

Interior Design

Material palettes, finishes, lighting, custom furniture and styling. A full interior brought together with a clear and cohesive aesthetic direction.

03

Art Curation

Sourcing and placement of original work and collected pieces, chosen to complete the interior and reflect the people who live or work in it.

04

Visualization & Rendering

Photoreal renders and walkthroughs in Enscape and D5 Render, so every decision can be seen, tested and approved before construction begins.
